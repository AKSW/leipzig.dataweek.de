15th Leipzig Semantic Web Day

Since 2009, Leipzig Semantic Web Day (LSWT) has brought together researchers, companies, and organizations to exchange ideas on the latest developments and applications of semantic technologies. With the rapid advances in Artificial Intelligence (AI), semantic technologies are becoming increasingly important and are gaining greater attention, particularly in business and industry.

The 15th Leipzig Semantic Web Day (#lswt2027) will take place on June 23, 2027, as part of Data Week Leipzig.

As in previous years, the event will showcase the latest developments and trends in semantic technologies and data. This year’s key topics include:

  • Large Language Models and Knowledge Graphs – Integration, GraphRAG, grounding, and hybrid AI
  • Trustworthy and Explainable AI – Hallucinations, provenance, quality, and governance
  • Semantic Technologies and AI for Industry and Research
  • Knowledge Graphs for Enterprise AI and Knowledge Management
  • Semantic Data Spaces, Data Products, and Industrial Data Ecosystems
  • Cybersecurity, Software Supply Chains, and Knowledge Graphs
  • FAIR Data, Metadata, and Intelligent Data Catalogs
  • Semantic Layers, Data Fabric, and Interoperable Data Architectures
  • Ontologies and Automated Knowledge Modeling with LLMs
  • Knowledge Graphs for Sustainability and Real-World Applications
  • Connected Society and Open Data

If you have any further questions, please feel free to contact the organizing team at: lswt@eccenca.com.
